How it works

Five steps. About a minute.

No sticky glue, no tricky eyeliner — just magnets that sandwich your natural lashes.

  1. Mascara firstCoat your natural lashes with a thin layer of your favourite mascara.
  2. Pick your pairEach eye uses two pieces: one goes above your lashes, one below.
  3. Top lashPlace the top piece over your natural lashes, close to the lash line.
  4. Bottom lashBring the bottom piece up underneath until the magnets click together.
  5. Adjust & goNudge into a comfortable position and enjoy your look.

Questions

Good to know

Are magnetic lashes safe?

Yes. Because there's no harsh glue on your skin, magnetic lashes don't damage your natural lashes the way glued-on falsies can.

How do I apply magnetic lashes?

Apply a thin coat of mascara to your natural lashes. Each eye uses two pieces from the box — one sits above your natural lashes and one below — and they clip together through your lashes with tiny magnets. See our step-by-step guide.

Are magnetic lashes suitable for sensitive eyes?

Yes — many of our customers with sensitive eyes wear them. Choose a mascara made for sensitive eyes and make sure your hands are clean before applying.

Do magnetic lashes need glue or eyeliner?

No. Roshy lashes need no glue and no magnetic eyeliner.
