Frequently asked questions

Are magnetic lashes safe?

Yes. Because there's no harsh glue on your skin, magnetic lashes don't damage your natural lashes the way glued-on falsies can.

How do I apply magnetic lashes?

Apply a thin coat of mascara to your natural lashes. Each eye uses two pieces from the box — one sits above your natural lashes and one below — and they clip together through your lashes with tiny magnets. See our step-by-step guide.

Are magnetic lashes suitable for sensitive eyes?

Yes — many of our customers with sensitive eyes wear them. Choose a mascara made for sensitive eyes and make sure your hands are clean before applying.

Do magnetic lashes need glue or eyeliner?

No. Roshy lashes need no glue and no magnetic eyeliner.

How long do they last?

With proper care, one set can be reused 30+ times. Store them in their box after each wear, and every few uses clean them gently with a cotton pad and micellar water.

Can I get a refund?

You can return your order within 14 days of receiving it — just contact us within that window to let us know. Return postage is paid by the customer. We refund your payment, including the original delivery charge, within 14 days of receiving the returned items. For health and hygiene reasons, lashes that have been unsealed can't be returned. Every product is checked individually before it's shipped. Full details are in our returns policy.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Unfortunately orders can't be cancelled or changed once they've been placed.

My tracking says delivered, but I haven't received my parcel.

Please contact the courier named in your dispatch email first. If you still can't find it, get in touch and we'll help.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard and other major cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Klarna and Revolut Pay. All payments are processed securely by Stripe — we never see or store your card details.
