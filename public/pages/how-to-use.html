Guide

How to use your Roshy lashes

Each set has an upper and a lower piece. You sandwich your natural lashes between them and the magnets do the rest.

Half lashes, step by step

  1. Apply a thin layer of your favourite mascara to your natural lashes. This gives the magnets something to grip.
  2. Choose your side. Each eye has two pieces in the box. Separate the pair that's stuck together — one piece goes above your natural lashes and one goes below.
  3. Place the top lash over your natural lashes, as close to the lash line as is comfortable.
  4. Place the bottom lash underneath your natural lashes and let the magnets connect.
  5. Adjust the lashes until they feel comfortable.
  6. Enjoy!

Tip: work on one eye at a time, and keep your hands clean. To remove, gently slide the top and bottom pieces apart — never pull.

Caring for your lashes

Store them in their box after every wear. After several uses, clean gently with a cotton pad and micellar water. Looked after well, one set lasts 30+ wears.
